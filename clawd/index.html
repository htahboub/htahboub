Privacy policy

The integration is used by its owner to work with email, calendars, files, documents, spreadsheets and contacts. Access is limited to the permissions the owner grants. It may read, create or update this data to fulfil the owner's requests and configured tasks.

Authorisation credentials are kept in the installed Google client's credential storage on the owner's computer. Data needed for tasks may be processed by the configured assistant, tools and AI service providers. Relevant task data may be retained in local files, conversation history and task records according to the owner's configuration. This information page does not collect account data, accept sign-ins or receive authorisation tokens, and uses no analytics or tracking scripts.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train general-purpose AI models. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke access in Google Account connections, remove credentials through the installed client and delete retained task records. Revoking access does not delete previously saved files or conversation history. Privacy and deletion enquiries can be sent using the contact link above.

Terms of use

This integration is for its owner's personal use only. It is not offered to visitors of this website. Connected services require the owner's authorisation and remain subject to their providers' applicable terms and policies.

The owner controls permissions, connected services and requested tasks. Automated results may contain errors; continuous availability and error-free operation are not guaranteed. The owner can stop using the integration and revoke access at any time.
