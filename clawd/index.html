Clawd personal assistant

A private, single-user personal assistant operated by Hamza Tahboub. It connects to the owner's Google account to help with email, scheduling, documents and other tasks requested by the owner. This is not a public sign-up service or a Google product.

Google account access

After the account owner authorises access, the assistant can read and manage Gmail messages, calendar events, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets and contacts within the granted permissions. Actions are performed to carry out the owner's requests and configured automations. The owner can revoke access at any time in Google Account connections.

Privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026.

This policy describes the Google account connection used by Clawd personal assistant. The assistant accesses account identity details and the Google service data needed for requested tasks, including messages and attachments, events, files and contacts. It may create or change data when instructed by the account owner.

Authorisation credentials are stored on the owner's computer using the installed Google client's credential storage. Retrieved data and task results may be processed by the owner's configured assistant, tools and AI service providers to fulfil requests. Local conversation history, task records and files may retain relevant data according to the owner's configuration. This public information page does not request Google authorisation, receive OAuth tokens or display private account content.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Google's API data is used to provide the personal-assistance features requested by the owner. Clawd's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke future Google access through Google Account connections, remove stored credentials through the installed client and delete locally retained task records. Revoking access does not itself delete previously saved local files or conversation history. For questions or deletion assistance, contact hamza.tahboub8@gmail.com.

Terms of use

Effective 31 October 2026.

Clawd personal assistant is intended for its owner's personal use. It is not offered as a public service. Google access requires the account owner's authorisation and remains subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

The owner controls the requested tasks, permissions and connected services. Automated results may contain errors and should be reviewed when appropriate. No guarantee of continuous availability or error-free operation is made. The owner can discontinue use and revoke connected-account access at any time.
